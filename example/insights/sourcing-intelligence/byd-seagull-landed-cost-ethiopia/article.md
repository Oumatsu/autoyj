## Overview

The BYD Seagull has become the reference vehicle for African EV importers asking the same foundational question: what does a credible, mass-market Chinese EV actually cost to land in my country?

The Seagull's China domestic price of approximately RMB 69,800–79,800 ($9,600–$11,000 USD) has been widely quoted in trade media and has driven significant importer interest across East Africa. What has not been widely explained is what happens between that factory-gate price and the moment a Seagull sits in a dealer's lot in Addis Ababa — ready for sale.

This article provides a complete, sourced cost breakdown for 2026, reflecting Ethiopia's current tax regime following the August 2024 reversal of EV incentives under Directive 1006/2024.

## The Cost Layers: China to Addis Ababa

### Layer 1: FOB Price (China Export)

The Seagull's China domestic price is a retail figure. Export pricing adds manufacturing costs for export compliance, OEM documentation, and margin to the exporting entity. In 2026, a Seagull bought through legitimate OEM export channels carries a **FOB Tianjin or FOB Shanghai** price in the range of **$11,500–$13,500 USD** for the standard range variant.

The gap between domestic retail ($9,600) and FOB export ($11,500–$13,500) reflects:
- Export documentation and compliance costs (now mandatory post-180-day rule)
- OEM export channel margin
- Currency conversion at prevailing rates

Traders offering Seagulls at below-FOB prices in 2026 should be treated as a compliance red flag under China's new 180-day export rule.

### Layer 2: Ocean Freight (FOB → Djibouti)

Ethiopia's primary vehicle import port is **Djibouti Port** — all landlocked Ethiopian imports clear Djibouti and transit by road or rail to Addis Ababa.

RoRo (Roll-on Roll-off) freight from Tianjin or Shanghai to Djibouti: **$750–$950 USD per vehicle** (2026 rates; lane-specific and subject to surcharges). Container shipping is typically 10–20% higher per vehicle due to loading and unloading costs.

Transit from Djibouti to Addis Ababa (road): approximately **$150–$250 USD** for standard trucking, varying with fuel surcharges and operator.

**Freight sub-total: $900–$1,200 USD**

### Layer 3: Marine Insurance

Standard CIF marine insurance for an EV shipment: **0.3–0.5% of vehicle value**. On a $12,500 FOB value: approximately **$40–$65 USD**.

### Layer 4: Ethiopian Customs Duties and Taxes

This is the most significant cost layer — and the one that changed materially in August 2024.

Ethiopia's current tax regime for imported EVs (as of Directive 1006/2024, August 2024) reintroduced taxes that had been exempted during the 2022–2024 incentive period.

| Tax Component | Rate | Applies To | Amount (on $13,000 CIF) |
|--------------|------|-----------|------------------------|
| Customs Duty | 5% | CIF value | ~$650 |
| Excise Tax | 30% | CIF + Duty | ~$4,095 |
| VAT | 15% | CIF + Duty + Excise | ~$2,662 |
| Sur-Tax | 10% | CIF + Duty | ~$1,365 |
| Withholding Tax | 3% | CIF + Duty | ~$410 |

**Total tax burden: approximately $9,182 USD** on a $13,000 CIF value.

*Note: Excise tax rates for EVs remain subject to policy interpretation by Ethiopian Customs. The 30% rate above reflects the reinstated schedule under Directive 1006/2024. Verify the current applicable rate with a licensed Ethiopian customs broker before finalising purchase commitments.*

### Layer 5: Port and Clearing Costs

Djibouti port handling and clearing fees: **$300–$500 USD** (varies with congestion surcharges, which have been elevated in 2025–2026 during Red Sea routing disruptions).

Ethiopian customs clearance and agent fees: **$150–$300 USD**.

**Port and clearing sub-total: $450–$800 USD**

## What This Means for Dealer Pricing

A BYD Seagull landed at $23,000 USD requires a dealer to price at **$26,000–$30,000 USD** to achieve a workable 15–25% gross margin after showroom costs, warranty reserves, and working capital carrying costs.

At 150 ETB/USD (the approximate market rate as of late 2025, following the Birr's managed float since July 2024), this translates to a **retail price range of ETB 3.9 million – ETB 4.5 million** ($26,000–$30,000 USD).

For comparison: a Toyota Corolla 2022 model in Ethiopia retails at approximately ETB 3.2–3.8 million. The Seagull, at current landed cost, is priced competitively with a late-model used Japanese ICE sedan — which is a reasonable market positioning given the Seagull's new-vehicle status and EV operating cost advantages.

## The Operating Cost Offset

The case for the Seagull is not purely about purchase price — it is about total cost of ownership over three to five years of commercial operation.

Ethiopian electricity costs average approximately ETB 1.40–2.50 per kWh (commercial rate, 2025 figures). At 30.1 kWh battery capacity and 250 km real-world range, the energy cost per kilometre is approximately **ETB 0.17–0.30/km**.

A comparable petrol sedan consuming 8 litres/100 km at Ethiopia's 2026 pump price of approximately ETB 85–95/litre costs approximately **ETB 6.80–7.60/km** in fuel alone.

The operating cost advantage: **ETB 6.50–7.30/km in fuel savings**. At 150 km/day for a commercial taxi operator, this represents **ETB 975–1,095/day** in fuel savings — roughly **ETB 28,000–32,000/month**.

Against a price premium of approximately ETB 0.7–1.0 million over an equivalent used ICE vehicle, **the Seagull's operating cost advantage recovers the price differential in approximately 24–36 months** under typical commercial taxi operating conditions.

## Sourcing Considerations

**Right-hand drive**: The BYD Seagull is produced in left-hand drive only. Ethiopia drives on the right, so this is not a compliance issue — but importers in Kenya, Tanzania, and South Africa (right-hand drive markets) cannot use the standard Seagull without conversion, which voids the warranty.

**180-day rule compliance**: Any Seagull manufactured in 2025 or 2026 exported within 180 days of registration must carry an OEM After-Sales Service Confirmation Document. Verify this document before any purchase commitment.

**Warranty in Ethiopia**: BYD does not have an official authorised dealer network in Ethiopia as of mid-2026. Warranty claims for independently imported Seagulls are not supported by BYD's official after-sales infrastructure. Importers should budget for independent service arrangements and parts stockpiling.
