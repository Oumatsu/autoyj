Module 01

ICE vs EV Cost Comparison

Overview

Building a convincing total cost of ownership (TCO) comparison between ICE and EV is the foundation of every corporate fleet proposal. African fleet operators need to see real numbers — not global averages — that reflect their local fuel prices, electricity tariffs, and maintenance costs.

A comprehensive TCO analysis includes purchase price, fuel/energy cost, maintenance, insurance, and residual value. In many African markets, the fuel saving alone can offset the higher upfront EV price within 18–24 months.

Key Cost Factors
  • Purchase Price: Include import duties, taxes, and shipping.
  • Fuel vs Energy: Compare petrol/diesel cost per km vs electricity cost per km at local tariffs.
  • Maintenance: EVs have 40–60% fewer moving parts than ICE vehicles.
  • Insurance: Premiums may differ — factor in local insurance class ratings.
  • Residual Value: Estimate based on battery degradation curves and local market demand.

Case Example

A logistics company in Kenya saved 25% on annual operating costs by switching to EVs for their last-mile delivery fleet. The savings came primarily from fuel cost elimination and reduced brake/powertrain maintenance.

Module 02

ROI Proposal Building

Overview

Corporate decision-makers don't buy vehicles — they buy financial outcomes. Your ROI proposal must translate technical specifications into clear business metrics: payback period, annual savings, and emissions reduction targets.

  • Present cost savings, emissions reduction, and incentives clearly to decision-makers.
  • Show 3-year and 5-year total cost projections with clear break-even points.
  • Include sensitivity analysis for fuel price volatility and electricity tariff changes.
  • Highlight non-financial benefits: brand positioning, regulatory compliance, and driver satisfaction.

Case Example

Corporate clients are more likely to adopt EV fleets when proposals show precise ROI and payback periods. A Nairobi-based courier company signed a 50-vehicle EV order after receiving a TCO analysis showing 28% cost reduction and 18-month payback.
