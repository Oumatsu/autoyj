Module 01

High-Voltage Safety

Overview

High-voltage (HV) systems in EVs operate at 400V to 800V — lethal levels that demand rigorous safety protocols. Every service centre handling Chinese EVs must implement standardized HV safety procedures before any technician touches a vehicle.

  • Follow strict PPE protocols: insulated gloves, face shield, HV-rated footwear.
  • Use only insulated tools rated for the vehicle's voltage level.
  • Implement lockout/tagout (LOTO) procedures before any battery service work.
  • Maintain a minimum two-person rule — never work on HV systems alone.

Case Example

Technicians trained in HV safety prevented accidents during battery replacements in Nigerian service centers. Proper LOTO procedures and insulated tooling eliminated electrical incidents entirely after implementation.

Module 02

Thermal Management

Overview

Battery temperature directly affects performance, lifespan, and safety. In African climates where ambient temperatures regularly exceed 35°C, proper thermal management is critical for preventing accelerated degradation and thermal runaway events.

  • Proper battery cooling extends lifespan and prevents thermal runaway.
  • Active liquid cooling systems maintain optimal operating range of 20-35°C.
  • Passive ventilation methods provide backup thermal regulation.
  • Regular thermal inspection should be part of every scheduled service.

Case Example

Service centers in hot climates (e.g., Sudan) installed temperature monitoring systems to prevent overheating, reducing battery-related service calls by 40% within six months.

Module 03

Diagnostic Tools

Overview

Modern EV diagnostics go far beyond traditional OBD-II scanning. Understanding battery management system (BMS) data, cell voltage balancing, and insulation resistance testing is essential for any service centre handling Chinese EV imports.

  • Use battery management software and OBD-II tools for fault detection and performance monitoring.
  • Monitor cell voltage variance — imbalance beyond 50mV indicates pending failure.
  • Track insulation resistance: values below 500Ω/V indicate compromised HV isolation.
  • Log thermal data over time to identify gradual degradation patterns.

Case Example

Early detection of cell imbalance in EVs prevented costly full-battery replacements in fleet operations. A Nairobi fleet operator saved $12,000 by replacing individual cells rather than the entire pack after routine BMS diagnostics flagged the issue.
