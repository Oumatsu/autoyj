Module 01

Certificate of Origin (COO)

Overview

The Certificate of Origin (COO) is a crucial international trade document that certifies the country of manufacture of your vehicles. In China-to-Africa EV exports, it is one of the most frequently requested documents by customs authorities across the continent.

  • COO certifies that the vehicle is manufactured in China and is essential for customs clearance in most African countries.
  • Includes information such as manufacturer details, VIN, production date, and country of origin.

Case Example

For an EV shipment to Kenya, a complete COO is required to claim reduced import duties under the African Continental Free Trade Area (AfCFTA) preferences.

Module 02

Battery Safety Certifications

Overview

Lithium battery safety certifications are non-negotiable for EV exports. Every shipment — whether by sea or air — must comply with international standards that govern the safe transport and operation of high-voltage battery systems.

Key International Standards
  • UN38.3 — Required for all lithium battery shipments (air and sea). Simulates altitude, temperature, vibration, shock, and short-circuit conditions.
  • IEC62133 — Safety standard for portable sealed alkaline and lithium cells.
  • MSDS (Material Safety Data Sheet) — Documents handling, storage, and emergency response procedures.

Case Example

EVs exported to Egypt must include battery certification documentation to pass port inspection. Missing or incomplete certifications can result in containers being held at port for weeks.

Module 03

Customs Clearance for African Ports

Overview

Each African port has unique import regulations, documentation requirements, and inspection procedures. Successful clearance depends on understanding these local nuances and preparing the correct paperwork in advance.

Typical Documentation Requirements
  • Certificate of Origin (COO)
  • Commercial Invoice
  • Packing List
  • Battery Safety Certificates (UN38.3, MSDS)
  • Import License (varies by country)
  • Bill of Lading (B/L)

Case Example

In Lagos, Nigeria, incomplete battery certification can delay clearance by 2–3 weeks, resulting in significant demurrage charges. Proper document preparation upfront eliminates these costly delays.
