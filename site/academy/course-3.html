Module 01

Online Showroom Setup

Overview

Your online showroom is the first impression most customers will have of your dealership. In African markets where mobile internet penetration is high but physical dealerships may be concentrated in capital cities, a well-crafted digital presence is your most cost-effective customer acquisition channel.

  • Create a localized website and product catalog tailored to African markets.
  • Showcase key vehicle specifications, features, and promotions with high-quality imagery.
  • Integrate real-time inventory management so customers see accurate stock levels.

Case Example

A South African dealership increased leads by 35% after launching a mobile-friendly catalog with real-time inventory updates, WhatsApp click-to-chat integration, and customer testimonials.

Module 02

Facebook Advertising

Overview

Facebook remains the dominant social platform across most African markets, making it the ideal channel for EV dealership advertising. With precise demographic and interest-based targeting, you can reach potential buyers at a fraction of traditional advertising costs.

  • Use pre-designed ad templates targeting local demographics and interests.
  • Optimize campaigns for impressions, clicks, and conversions based on your sales funnel stage.
  • Retarget website visitors with dynamic vehicle inventory ads.

Case Example

Targeting Nairobi residents aged 25-45 with mid-range EV ads increased showroom visits by 20% within the first month of campaign launch.

Module 03

WhatsApp Business Automation

Overview

WhatsApp is the de facto communication platform across Africa. Automating your WhatsApp Business account transforms it from a simple messaging tool into a 24/7 sales engine that nurtures leads, shares inventory, and books test drives without additional staff.

  • Automate customer engagement with welcome messages, vehicle catalog sharing, and booking reminders.
  • Set up quick replies for frequently asked questions about range, pricing, and delivery.
  • Use broadcast lists to notify prospects about new arrivals and promotions.

Case Example

A dealership in Lagos reduced response time to inquiries from 24 hours to under 30 minutes after implementing WhatsApp Business automation with catalog integration.

✦

Service Integration Point: Automation setup and training for WhatsApp Business integrated with your management system are included in our dealer enablement package.

Module 04

Local SEO for African Markets

Overview

When African buyers search for EVs, they turn to Google Maps and local search. Optimizing for these platforms ensures your dealership appears when potential customers are actively looking, making local SEO one of the highest-ROI digital investments you can make.

  • Optimize Google Maps and local search listings to improve visibility.
  • Include relevant keywords, local contact information, and operational hours.
  • Collect and respond to customer reviews to build trust and improve rankings.

Case Example

SEO improvements helped a Kenyan dealer rank top for 'EV for sale Nairobi', increasing web leads by 50% within three months of implementation.

✦

Service Integration Point: SEO audit reports, content templates, and guidance for local market optimization are provided to help you dominate your local search results.
